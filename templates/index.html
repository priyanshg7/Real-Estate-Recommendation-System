End-to-End Data Science & Machine Learning Pipeline

A step-by-step summary of the data extraction, preprocessing, outlier detection, MICE imputation, and exploratory data analysis across all 8 notebooks.

1

Notebook 1: Flat Listings Cleaning & Scraping Normalization

Extracted raw flat listings from raw_Data/flats.csv. Cleaned messy price strings (Crores vs Lakhs), extracted sector numbers via regex, processed carpet/super built-up areas, and formatted bathroom & balcony counts into structured integer types.

2

Notebook 2: Independent House Listings Cleaning

Cleaned raw house listings from raw_Data/house.csv. Standardized rate per sq.ft, floor numbers allowed, land areas (Sq. Yards / Sq. Meters to Sq.Ft conversion), and consolidated address descriptions.

3

Notebook 3: Merging Flat and House Datasets

Merged the cleaned flats and houses datasets into a unified schema, ensuring column consistency and preserving property identifiers across 3,900+ listings.

4

Notebook 4: Combined Data Preprocessing & Sector Mapping

Standardized Gurgaon sectors (e.g. 'Sector 102', 'Sector 85', 'Dwarka Expressway', 'Golf Course Road'), resolved multi-sector inconsistencies, and computed estimated area metrics.

5

Notebook 5: Feature Engineering & Luxury Scoring

Extracted 50+ luxury amenity tags (swimming pool, clubhouse, tennis court, security, etc.) into a weighted Luxury Score (0 to 100+). One-hot encoded additional rooms (study room, servant room, store room, pooja room) and categorical furnishing status (0=Unfurnished, 1=Semi-Furnished, 2=Furnished).

6

Notebook 6: Outlier Treatment & Missing Value Imputation (OT & MVI)

Detected and removed price anomalies (e.g. ultra-low prices < 5 Lakhs, abnormally high price/sqft outliers), filtered impossible bedroom-to-bathroom ratios, and imputed missing built-up areas using linear regression / median category models to generate OT_MVI.csv.

7

Notebook 7: Univariate Statistical Analysis

Analyzed distribution shape, skewness, kurtosis, and histograms for price, rate per sq.ft, area, and BHK configurations. Identified heavy right-skewed price distribution log-transformations.

8

Notebook 8: Multivariate & Correlation Exploratory Analysis

Evaluated multi-variable interactions: price vs built-up area across property types, luxury index correlation with property rates, and sector-wise premium pricing tiers.
